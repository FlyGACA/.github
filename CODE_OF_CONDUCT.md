# Code of Conduct

FlyGACA is a community of pilots, students, instructors and developers. We want it to be welcoming and professional — the same standard you would expect on a flight deck.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, in English and in Arabic.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent and help newcomers learn.
- Put accuracy and safety first, and say so when you are unsure.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Knowingly sharing false or unsafe aviation information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **i@flygaca.com**. All reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.

## ميثاق السلوك

نلتزم بالاحترام والمهنية في جميع النقاشات، ونقدّم الدقة والسلامة على كل شيء. للإبلاغ عن أي مخالفة: i@flygaca.com
